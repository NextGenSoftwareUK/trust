Clear explanations for the trust roles and documents.

These cards keep the language calm and human so users can understand the form before they start entering details.

Express private trust

An express private trust is a trust created intentionally by a settlor for a defined purpose and named beneficiaries.

Settlor

The person who creates the trust and provides the initial trust property.

Trustee

The person or people who manage the trust and follow the trust deed.

Beneficiary

The person or class of people who may benefit from the trust arrangements.

Protector

An optional oversight role that can add an extra layer of control to the structure.

Signing process

The final execution step where the parties sign in the correct order and location.
