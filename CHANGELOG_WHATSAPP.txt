SovereignTrust — Release Notes
================================

v1.2.0 — 5 July 2026
-----------------------

HOMEPAGE & MESSAGING

• Hero headline rewritten — clearer, more compelling opening that speaks directly to the visitor
• New "Why Sovereign Trust" section added — 8 cards answering the key questions: what it is, why it exists, who it's for, what problem it solves, what makes it different, and why you can trust it
• Disclaimer rewritten — now clearly states what the platform does and directs users to seek professional advice where needed
• Call to action updated from "Begin Your Trust" to "Begin Your Sovereign Trust"
• "What's Included" grid completed — Auto-Save Draft and Instant Download cards added
• WIP banner removed — site is now presented as live and complete

LEELA AI — CONVERSATIONAL ONBOARDING

• Leela now greets every new user with a short conversation before the builder opens
• She asks 3 questions: what you want to protect, who should benefit, and what assets you plan to hold
• Based on your answers she recommends the right trust structure
• She then pre-fills the Trust Purpose field for you — highlighted in gold so you can see it immediately
• Returning users and existing trusts skip straight to the builder

DASHBOARD

• Loading spinner added while your trust profiles are being fetched
• Two new Coming Soon modules added: Deed of Variation and Beneficiary Notification

DOWNLOADS

• Word document (DOCX) download now available alongside the PDF
• Both files contain the full trust deed — PDF for signing, DOCX for editing with your advisors

GENERAL

• Version number (v1.2.0) now shown in the bottom-right corner of every page
